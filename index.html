A Historical Thought Experiment

The American Sides Quiz

★ ★ ★
If you had lived in British North America during the Revolutionary and Constitutional eras, where might your instincts have placed you?

Before You Begin

This is a long-form historical personality quiz. It is intended for curious high-school students, college students, and adults. It is written in a serious tone, but it is ultimately a game.

You will answer 50 questions divided into two sections:

  • Section I — Patriot or Loyalist: 25 questions concerning the Revolution and the question of continued allegiance to Britain.
  • Section II — Federalist or Anti-Federalist: 25 questions concerning the Constitution, federal power, state authority, representation, and individual liberties.
Important: There are no correct answers. Do not try to “win” the quiz. Do not try to guess what answer produces which result. Simply choose the answer that most closely reflects your own instinct in the situation described.

Some questions are deliberately subtle. Some may leave you genuinely torn. Pick the answer that feels most natural to you. Your final result is based on the pattern of your answers—not on any individual question.

Historically, these labels were more complicated than a modern four-letter quiz can capture. People could be Patriots on one issue and cautious about independence on another; similarly, people who opposed the Constitution could differ substantially in their reasons for doing so.
